# Sentinel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

## What Sentinel processes

Sentinel is a personal home-automation assistant. When you use the Sentinel Alexa skill, Amazon sends the spoken command to the skill. The skill forwards the command and a technical user identifier to the Sentinel relay so the command can reach your own Sentinel installation.

## Storage and retention

Commands are held by the relay only long enough to deliver them to Sentinel, for a maximum of 120 seconds, and are then deleted. Sentinel device settings and integration credentials are stored on the user’s own device.

## Sharing

Sentinel does not sell personal data. Amazon and Cloudflare process limited request data as necessary to provide their respective services.

## Your choices

You can stop using the skill at any time by disabling it in the Alexa app. You can also remove locally stored Sentinel data from the Sentinel application.
